# Phase 7-2 — Customer wording and disclaimer audit

This checkpoint creates a commercial wording and disclaimer audit for the paid
Covered Call Simulator.

It is add-only and makes no dashboard or engine change.

The audit checks launch-readiness wording for:

- options risk;
- education/scenario-analysis framing;
- financial-advice disclaimer language;
- historical-data caution;
- regime detection as probabilistic guidance, not an oracle;
- avoiding forbidden performance claims such as guaranteed profit or risk-free returns.

Run:

`app\run_paid_simulator_phase7_2_customer_wording_disclaimer_audit_check.py`
